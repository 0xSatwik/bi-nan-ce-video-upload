💡What is Binance Word of the Day?

Binance Word of the Day (WOTD) is a daily word puzzle game offered by Binance where users guess a crypto-related word to earn rewards. It's similar to Wordle but focuses on cryptocurrency terminology. Each day, a new word is released and you have limited attempts to guess it.

Use this solver to maximize your chances of finding the answer quickly. Simply enter your guesses, mark the results with colors, and let our algorithm suggest the optimal next moves based on remaining possibilities.

❓Frequently Asked Questions

What word lengths are supported?▼
The solver supports words from 3 to 8 letters long, covering all possible Binance WOTD puzzle variations.
What is Hard Mode?▼
Hard Mode requires any revealed hints (green and yellow letters) to be used in subsequent guesses. This makes the puzzle more challenging but helps narrow down answers faster.
What's the difference between "All Words" and "Most Likely"?▼
"Most Likely" shows only common words that are likely to be puzzle answers, while "All Words" includes the full dictionary of valid guesses including obscure words.
Is this solver free to use?▼
Yes! This Binance WOTD Solver is completely free to use. No sign-up or payment required.
