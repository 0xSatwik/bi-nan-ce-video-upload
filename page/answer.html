What is Binance Word of the Day (WODL)?

Binance Word of the Day (WODL) is an exciting daily word puzzle game offered by Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange. Similar to Wordle, players guess crypto-related words to earn rewards. Each day features a new theme with words ranging from 3 to 8 letters.

The WODL game rewards players with crypto tokens for correctly guessing the words. Our page provides verified answers updated daily so you never miss out on your rewards!

How to Play Binance WODL

  1. Open the Binance App and navigate to the WODL game section
  2. Select your word length — puzzles are available for 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 letter words
  3. Enter your guess and check the color feedback (green = correct position, yellow = wrong position, grey = not in word)
  4. Use our answers above to reveal the correct words and claim your rewards!

Today's Binance WODL Theme (Feb 8, 2026)

Binance updates the WODL theme regularly, usually on a weekly basis. Each theme focuses on different aspects of the cryptocurrency world — from blockchain technology to DeFi protocols, stablecoins, and more. The answers on this page are automatically updated to match the current active theme.
